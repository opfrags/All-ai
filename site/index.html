Straight answers.

The questions owners ask us most. Still unsure? We answer on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
What can it do?

Paperwork like invoices, quotes and records. It can build and update your website and a simple app, and take on other computer jobs you give it. If you are not sure it can do something, ask us on WhatsApp.

Is my data safe?

We take care with your information, and your AI employee only works on what you give it. Ask us on WhatsApp how we handle your business data before you share anything.

Do I need tech skills?

No. You choose a plan, then tell your AI employee what you need in plain words. You don't need to install or learn anything.

What does it cost?

Helper is $79 per month and Teammate is $200 per month, for small businesses. Expert is $300 per month, for small and mid-sized businesses. You can also start with a business audit: apply on our audit page, no price is listed. Ad spend, third-party subscriptions and licences are not included.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from your dashboard, or message us on WhatsApp and we will help. Ask before you start so you know how it works.

What language does it use?

You can message us in English. If you would rather use Shona or Ndebele, ask us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible.
